# ARCSTUDIO91 Content Quality Skill

## Mission
Create original, useful architecture education for @arcstudio91 using free/open-source tools only. Do not require paid subscriptions, image-generation credits, payment cards, or proprietary paid APIs. Never claim a carousel was published unless the platform confirms it.

## Free production stack
- **Python + Pillow**: compose and export each slide independently at 1080×1350 JPG and PNG master.
- **Wikimedia Commons**: preferred source for real reference photography only when the specific file page confirms its licence and attribution requirements. Save source URL, creator, licence, and access date.
- **Openverse**: optional discovery index for openly licensed media; verify the original source and licence before use.
- **Inkscape**: free vector editing for clean diagrams and simple architectural linework.
- **Blender**: free/open-source 3D tool for original concept massing/rendering when a genuine rendered concept is needed; label it “concept visual”, never a real project photo.
- **GIMP**: free image editing and final visual checks.
- **GitHub Actions**: free-tier scheduled builds where quota permits; do not assume unlimited usage. Keep publishing disabled until credentials and account mapping are deliberately verified.
- **FFmpeg**: optional free tool for future motion/video exports; not needed for a static carousel.

## Research and originality
1. Use the supplied Instagram profile only if public access works. If it cannot be accessed, say so and rely on the stated brand principles without inventing profile observations.
2. Research broad worldwide architecture context using freely accessible sources (e.g., architecture publications, public university resources, standards/guidance from official bodies). Record source URLs and publication dates. Research informs context; do not copy the source's drawings, plans, renders, project compositions, captions, or distinctive details.
3. Create a genuinely original concept and original Marathi + English wording. Rotate typologies globally: homes, apartments, villas, offices, commercial, resorts, hotels, cafés/restaurants, retail, education, healthcare, public space, landscape, and adaptive reuse.
4. Check the recent concept log before drafting. Avoid repeating recent concepts, headlines, images, material lists, and sentence structures. If no reliable history is available, state that repetition checking is limited.
5. Verify factual claims. Separate broadly accepted principles from project-specific assumptions. Do not present design guidance as construction instructions; recommend site-specific review by a qualified local architect/engineer when relevant.

## Quality gates before export
- Exactly four **separate** portrait slides; never output a 2×2 collage.
- Each slide is full-bleed 4:5, 1080×1350, with legible typography and safe margins.
- Slide 1: one original concept and one short headline.
- Slide 2: one practical climate or space insight.
- Slide 3: one distinct safety, durability, or maintenance insight.
- Slide 4: plain-language materials/sourcing, short service list, and +91 9921894495 at the top. Contact details must not appear on slides 1–3.
- The small handle arcstudio91 appears at the top of every slide.
- Style stays ultra-minimal, modern, natural and functional. Avoid ornate luxury styling, gold decoration, icon rows, tiny text, decorative cards, crowded infographics, and repeated slogans.
- Use only media with verified reuse permissions. Include required creator/licence attribution in caption/source notes. If no suitable licensed photo is available, use original linework or a Blender-created concept visual and label it clearly; never invent a photo licence or imply a concept image is a completed project.
- Do not stretch or distort photos. Check contrast, spelling, Marathi glyph rendering, alignment, crop, duplicate images, source metadata, and all four exports.
- Create 1080×1350 JPGs and PNG masters, plus a preview contact sheet. The contact sheet is for review only and is not one of the four publishable slides.
- Provide a bilingual Marathi + English caption listing: residential and commercial architecture; resorts/hotels/cafés; interiors/spatial planning; sustainable design; renovation/adaptive reuse; and material guidance.
- Add concise research/source notes and identify any illustrative images. Do not make unsupported claims such as guaranteed 100-year life; longevity is an aspiration, not a guarantee.

## Free-tool fallback policy
If image generation is rate-limited or unavailable, do not retry repeatedly and do not block the entire content workflow. Use Pillow-built typography/layout, original vector diagrams from Inkscape/SVG, or a simple Blender concept model when feasible. If a high-quality result cannot be made from available free assets, stop safely and report the limitation instead of using an unlicensed image or lowering quality silently.

## Publishing safety
- Generate and inspect the package before considering publication.
- Never place tokens, passwords, cookies, or secrets in source files, commits, logs, captions, or chat.
- Keep auto-publishing disabled until the user has intentionally configured and verified the correct Meta Page and Instagram account.
- Record platform confirmation/IDs and verify the live post. If the result is ambiguous, mark for review and inspect the platform before retrying.
